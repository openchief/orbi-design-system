Shape, elevation & focus

8px radius everywhere, 2px soft borders, layered shadows, and a 4px tinted focus ring.

--shadow-card

Cards, tiles, tables.

--shadow-raised

Menus, popovers, modals.

--focus-ring

Keyboard focus indicator.

Motion

A single 0.15s ease transition handles colour, border and transform changes. Animations respect prefers-reduced-motion.
