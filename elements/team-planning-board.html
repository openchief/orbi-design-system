Team planning board

A monday.com-style planning table: groups of work items with owner, status, priority and timeline.

Board

Everything is editable. Click an item name, group name or column header to rename it; click an owner, status, priority or timeline to change it; use ▸ to open subitems and the row buttons to add a subitem or delete (click twice to confirm). Groups can be recoloured, deleted or added.

Timeline

A date range drawn against a four-week window. Click it to set start and end dates, or clear them.

Item with subitems
